## Full description

Out of all the photos you take, keep the moments that truly matter.

We take dozens of photos a day, yet only look back at a few. Keepsake helps you pick the moments you want to hold on to, write down the story behind them, and come back to them years later.

📷 Pick a few each day
• Pick up right where you left off, so you never have to remember how far you got
• Missed a few? We'll bring them back in Missed photos
• Get a gentle reminder on the schedule that suits you

✏️ Add the story behind each photo
• Write a short note about how that moment felt
• The place fills in from the photo itself, with no location permission needed

📚 Albums your way
• Make albums for trips, family, or seasons, however you like to look back
• Albums point to your originals instead of copying them, so they never take up extra space
• See where your photos were taken on the Album Map
• On this day quietly brings back photos from years ago

🧹 Let go of the rest, lightly
• When you're done, look over the photos you didn't keep and clear them only if you want to

👨‍👩‍👧 Shared albums for the whole family
Photos are better together. Make an album with your parents, family, or friends and open it together anytime.
• Join with an invite code and add the photos each of you kept
• Family members without a subscription can view and add photos too. They just need to sign in
• Everyone shares the album's storage, with each member chipping in a little

🔒 Keepsake Secure Storage (optional subscription)
Secure Storage subscriptions are coming soon. Thanks for your patience!
• Keep the photos and videos in your secure albums safe, even if you delete them from your phone or lose it
• Photos you upload on iPhone show up on Android too (sign in with Google)
• Space saver keeps photos at a size that still looks sharp on a phone (2048px on the long edge) to save storage. Keep originals for the ones that matter most
• Create shared albums with a Secure Storage subscription

🌱 Start without an account
Picking, notes, albums, maps, and cleanup are all free. Your memories stay on this device. Sign in to back up notes, places, and albums for free and pick up on a new phone.
