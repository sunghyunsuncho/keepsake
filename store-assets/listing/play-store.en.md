## Short description

Keep the photos that matter, with the notes and places that make them yours.

## Full description

We take many photos, but return to only a few. Keepsake helps you choose the photos you truly want to keep and save their notes and places alongside them.

Pick up right where you last reviewed your library, group memories into albums, and review the rest before clearing space. Core features work free without an account. Sign in to back up memories for free, or choose Secure Storage to keep original photos and videos safe across phones.
