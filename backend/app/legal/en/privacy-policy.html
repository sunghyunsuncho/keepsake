Keepsake Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience only. In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the English and Korean versions, the Korean version shall prevail.

This Privacy Policy explains how Atelier Labs ("we," "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use the Keepsake app (the "Service"), and how you can exercise your rights. We have established this policy in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea so that we can protect your personal information and respond promptly to any concerns you may have. If you use the Service without signing in, we do not collect any personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Information from sign-in

Sign-in providerInformation we receive

Information collected while you use the Service (when signed in)

  • Entries: notes, place names, the date, time and time zone a photo was taken, a photo's location coordinates and dimensions, your albums and the entries in them, and your picking history (dates and how many photos you picked)
  • Photos in Safe Albums (paid subscribers only): the original and a viewing copy for "Original Quality" albums, or a viewing copy only for "Space Saver" albums
  • Subscription information: subscription status, expiry date and payment event records. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Apple and Google; we never receive them.
  • Photo link information: the identifiers of photos in your device's photo library, and a device key (a hash of your device's unique value combined with your account)
  • Notification information: your push notification token and device type (iOS or Android)
  • Information collected automatically: your storage usage and photo count, and, when the app encounters an error, your device model, OS version, app version and error details

Information kept only on your device (never sent to us)

  • Entries you created before signing in, your picking reminder settings, the notifications you have received (the most recent 100), and cleanup suggestions

3. Retention and Deletion

  • We delete personal information without delay when you delete your account or when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for. We delete the database records on our servers and the files in our storage so that they cannot be recovered.
  • When you delete an entry in the app, we delete that entry and its photos from our servers.
  • When you remove a photo from a Safe Album, we delete it from our servers if it is no longer in any Safe Album.
  • When your subscription ends, we delete only the photos on our servers, 30 days later. Your entries, albums, photo link information and account are kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately delete your entries, photos, photo link information, account information, subscription information and push notification tokens from our servers, and we also delete your customer record at RevenueCat.
  • Signing out leaves your information on our servers, so you can pick up where you left off when you sign in again.
  • Where the law requires us to keep certain information, we keep it for the required period. Under the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, records of contracts, cancellations, payments and the supply of goods are kept for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints and dispute resolution for 3 years. For this reason, payment event records are kept for 5 years from the date we receive them, even after you delete your account, and are then deleted automatically.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties for any purpose other than those described in Section 2, except:

  • where you have given your prior consent; or
  • where a law enforcement agency requests it in accordance with the law.

6. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access, correction and deletion: you can view, edit and delete your entries directly in the app.
  • Deleting photos: removing a photo from a Safe Album deletes it from our servers.
  • Suspension of processing: you can ask us to stop processing your personal information.
  • Opting out of notifications: you can turn off notification permission in your device settings.
  • Deleting your account: go to [Settings > Account > Delete Account] in the app, or email us.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at atelierlabs.kr@gmail.com and we will act on your request without delay. Please note that deleting your account does not cancel your app store subscription; cancel it in your device's subscription settings.

7. How We Keep Your Information Secure

  • Our photo storage is never public. Photos are uploaded and downloaded only through signed links that expire after a short time.
  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Each user's data is stored separately, and the contents of notes and places are kept out of our server logs and error reports.
  • Access is limited to authorized personnel on a least-privilege basis.

9. Photos and Location Information

  • Photo library access: we use photo library permission to let you pick photos to keep, show the photos you have kept, clean up (delete) the photos you confirm, and save originals from the Safe Library back to your device's photo library. The Service does not copy your photos; it points to the originals in your device's photo library.
  • Automatic place names: if a photo has location coordinates, the app sends them to your device's built-in address lookup to find the place name. This is handled by Apple's servers on iOS and Google's servers on Android. We never receive this information.
  • The Service does not ask for permission to access your device's current location.

10. App Notifications

  • If you turn on picking reminders, they are scheduled and shown directly by your device and never go through our servers. You can turn them off at any time under [Settings > Device Library > Picking Reminders].
  • We send signed-in users push notifications that they need in order to use the Service, such as when a subscription ends or photos are about to be deleted.
  • We do not send marketing messages.

11. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 14. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 14, we will delete it without delay.
